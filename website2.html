Formula 1 (F1) is the highest level of international motor racing. It features the fastest racing cars in the world. F1 races are held on famous circuits in different countries. Teams like Ferrari, Mercedes, and McLaren compete for the championship. Each team has two skilled drivers who race throughout the season. The sport combines speed, technology, and teamwork. Engineers constantly improve the cars to make them faster and safer.
